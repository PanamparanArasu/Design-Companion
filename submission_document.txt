DESIGN COMPANION — AI PROTOTYPE SUBMISSION PACKAGE
===================================================

CHALLENGE 1: DESIGN COMPANION
Develop an AI prototype that brainstorms layouts, generates UI copy, or summarizes usability feedback.

---------------------------------------------------
1. SHORT SUMMARY (83 words)
---------------------------------------------------
Design Companion is an AI-powered triage and design workbench that transforms raw, unorganized usability session notes into actionable, heuristic-mapped fix lists, layout exploration briefs, and UX copy options.

Built with Next.js and Shadcn UI, it features an automated classification engine mapping feedback to Nielsen’s 10 Heuristics with a deterministic severity score (2 × Blockers + 1.5 × Notes + Spread). It accepts Zoom/Teams .vtt transcripts, displays clean sticky-note feedback cards, and includes dynamic prompt generators for instant wireframing and microcopy iterations.

3. PUBLIC DATASET & INPUT FORMATS
---------------------------------------------------
- Sample Dataset: 20 usability testing session notes across 6 participants evaluating digital ticketing flows.
- Transcript Compatibility: Automated parsing of Zoom and MS Teams (.vtt, .srt, .txt, .csv, .xlsx) meeting transcripts.

---------------------------------------------------
4. KEY PROTOTYPE HIGHLIGHTS
---------------------------------------------------
1. Automated Heuristic Triage:
   Scans session transcripts, removes moderator chatter, maps pain points to Nielsen's 10 Heuristics, and ranks issues by impact.

2. Sticky Note Synthesis Board:
   Visualizes research findings on clean #FDC770 golden-yellow sticky notes over a pitch-black OLED #000000 canvas.

3. Layout Exploration Studio:
   Generates structured prompts for 4 distinct wireframe layout directions given screen goals and mobile constraints.

4. UX Copy Studio:
   Drafts clear, plain-language error messages, empty states, and button CTAs under strict word-count limits.
